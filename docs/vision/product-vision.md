# Product Vision

## What is Columbus AI?

Columbus AI is a client management and business automation platform designed for small and medium-sized service businesses.

The platform combines CRM functionality, client communication, onboarding workflows, document management, automation, reporting, and AI assistance into a single system.

## Primary Goal

Reduce administrative work for business owners and provide a clear operating system for managing leads, clients, tasks, communications, and business processes.

## Core Principles

1. Simple before powerful.
2. Every screen should answer "What needs attention?"
3. Automation should reduce clicks.
4. The platform should be usable by non-technical business owners.
5. Mobile and desktop experiences must both be first-class.
6. AI should assist workflows, not replace visibility.

## Target Customer

Small service businesses with 1–25 employees.

Examples:

* Estheticians
* Recovery housing operators
* Consultants
* Agencies
* Home service companies
* Professional service providers

## Core Modules

1. CRM
2. Lead Management
3. Client Portal
4. Admin Portal
5. Documents
6. Messaging
7. Automations
8. Reporting
9. AI Assistant

## Success Definition

A business owner can log in and immediately know:

* New leads
* Pending tasks
* Client status
* Outstanding documents
* Messages requiring response
* Revenue pipeline
